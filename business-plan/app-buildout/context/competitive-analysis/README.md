## Verdict

There is no large active app called Trippy. The huge app is TripIt (reported about 22M travelers, Pro $49 a year), a post-booking organizer. Trippy at letsgetrippy.com is a small new group-trip app with chat, voting, expense splitting and a chat-to-plan AI. Tripsy is a polished Apple-only organizer, and Wanderlog is the closest planner. AI planners (Layla, now Expedia-owned, Mindtrip, Wanderboat, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT and others) share the same weaknesses: invented or stale places, no per-fact sources, one-shot itineraries, weak group planning, no price tracking after the plan, paywalls before value, and gated offline. Hermi should not fight on live inventory or generic chat planning. It wins on proof (evidence labels and "Verify this plan"), free couple collaboration, fare watching tied to the plan, honest billing and commerce, and an easy switch from TripIt, Tripsy and Wanderlog.
