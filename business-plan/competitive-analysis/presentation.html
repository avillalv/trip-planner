Competitor 1 of 5

TripIt: the huge post-booking organizer

About 22M travelersPro $49 a yeariOS, Android, webReported, verify

Excels at

  • Forward an email, get a master itinerary
  • Pro flight alerts, often before the airline
  • Fare-drop and refund alerts users say pay for Pro
  • SAP Concur distribution and brand trust

Lacks

  • Sharing is view-only, no co-editing
  • No AI planner, lodging shortlist or voting
  • Price tracking only after you book
  • Alerts and offline sit behind Pro; UI feels dated

Wayfold does better

  • Plan together before booking, free for couples
  • Fare tracking tied to the plan, with sources
  • Stays shortlist with voting; presentation mode
  • Imports TripIt trips for a smooth switch

Adjacent giant, not a head-on rival. Pitch the stage before booking; do not claim to replace TripIt.

Category weaknesses

Seven things AI planners keep getting wrong

1

Invented or stale places

Closed restaurants, hotels that do not exist.

Wayfold: ungrounded places are dropped.

2

No per-fact sources

Place cards, not "where from, and when".

Wayfold: source and date on every fact.

3

One-shot itineraries

Plans do not adapt and can reshuffle.

Wayfold: drafts are previews; you accept.

4

Weak groups

A chat tag or a paid share link.

Wayfold: shortlist, hearts, free for couples.

5

No price tracking after the plan

A price is shown once, then forgotten.

Wayfold: fare history and alerts on the trip.

6

Paywall before value

The useful part is locked until you pay.

Wayfold: a free first draft and 12 credits a month.

7

Offline gated or missing

Pro only, limited, or absent.

Wayfold: offline reading on every tier.

Where Wayfold loses

Be candid about the gaps and skip the wrong fights

Where we trail

  • Email forwarding and flight status: TripIt and Tripsy lead; both land in phase 2
  • Group chat and chat-to-plan AI: Trippy
  • Android app and Apple polish: Tripsy, Wanderlog, TripIt
  • Live co-editing: Wanderlog feels live; Wayfold polls every 15 to 30 seconds
  • Brand and reach: Wayfold has zero users

Fights to avoid

  • Live inventory and booking: Google, Expedia, Booking win
  • One-shot "plan me a trip" chat: ChatGPT and Google win
  • Place and review data: do not build a database
  • Paid search on "AI trip planner": they out-spend us
  • Lifetime-price wars: never offer lifetime

Stay where trust, collaboration and follow-through matter more than inventory or reach.

Top gaps to exploit

Six gaps users complain about, ranked

  1. Paywalled basicsOffline and alerts are locked at TripIt, Wanderlog and Layla. Offer free offline and honest pricing.
  2. AI you cannot checkInvented venues and dates. Show the source and date on every fact.
  3. Groups cannot decide in one appChat, Splitwise and polls live apart. Shortlist, hearts and, in phase 2, polls and cost splits.
  4. Fares only after you bookOnly post-booking refund alerts exist at TripIt. Watch the fare from the plan and alert on drops.
  5. Trial and cancellation trapsSurprise charges at Wanderlog and Layla. Trial on annual only, clear dates, one-tap cancel.
  6. Hard to switchImport is fragile or Pro only. Calendar file and pasted bookings in, free Trip Pass for the first import.
Reported, verifyRanked by how often and how angrily the complaint appears in reviews.

Next moves

Six moves to win users

The full plan is in win-plan.md.

  1. Make the evidence label the heroVisible on every AI item, tappable, with a stale flag. Demo it first.
